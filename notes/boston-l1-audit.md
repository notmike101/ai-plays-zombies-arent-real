# Boston L1 autonomous play audit, November 20

## Decision

Retain the incumbent. First recover an actor-specific baseline and make autonomous preparation reliably produce evaluable battles. Current data identify failures at several successive steps. They cannot assign a fraction of lost civilians to each step. A friend's training duration has no comparable starting conditions, gameplay contract, denominator or hardware-time record.

Completion filtering, older framing and replay all limit transfer. Layout-free frozen cohorts in that reconstruction yielded one eligible natural finish in 34 attempts, at 0%. Keep retries, pauses, crashes and incomplete attempts in the denominator. A new launcher seed, board fingerprint or copy name cannot certify a new native outbreak. Reserved copies have reproduced discovery or earlier evaluation outbreaks.
* Independently reconstructed 108 training attempts (both boundary studies, opening timing and capacity rounds 0–2). 91 retries, 15 planning timeouts, two natural wins, zero at 35%. The first three completed studies contributed 24 frozen attempts, 17 retries and seven timeouts. Distinguish an official natural outcome from the observed score at an early retry.

## 2. Preparation and opportunities dominate the observed failure path

* 13/93 starts had occupied shelter, 35/93 had confirmed spends and 36/93 had nonempty deployment logs. The counters measure different events. In 15 planning timeouts READY was legal and declined 698 times. Six ended with no blocker, five with an unfinished gesture, four over budget. End-of-episode legality cannot explain every earlier decision. Early READY can also start an unprepared battle.
* Native polygon events establish both successful edits and unintended changes. One historical rectangle selection deleted an existing four-corner polygon, replaced it with five corners, and created another rectangle (tracker, boundary 239417). Completed non-rectangular zones exist. A count of vertices, wall cells or selected corner actions cannot validate intent. Preserve pre/post polygon identity and vertices, clear initial draft, each corner increment, commit receipt, budget and playable land. Score free-form polygons on their own geometry. Preserve the model's opportunity to undo or reshape.
* In 16 distinct occupied training outbreaks from duration and geometry studies, 12 lacked an embedded defensive building, 11 of those breached (tracker). Active nearby garrison kill counters were observed in another case ending at an 8.81% retry. Presence, firing and effectiveness are different observations. Special activation requires the same selected building to change from no ability to the exact native HUD choice. No event-specific evidence supports a broad claim that all special choices work on every board.
* Stock reductions establish spends. Native deploy events and impact events separately establish release and effect. Missile civilian cost requires an untruncated native event stream, unique consistent impact IDs, valid civilian losses and a matching elapsed-time step. No confirmed positive missile civilian loss appears in the bounded 108-game cohort. The earlier footprint study matched just 19/24 shots to outcomes. Avoid attributing nearby population loss to a particular cast.

## 3. Reward targets and the PPO update favor different time scales

* Summed recorded components before bootstrap had medians of 0 at planning timeout, -0.44838 at retry and -0.20854 in the two native wins. Actor 2e654bf5875b7089 earned +0.65050 terminal for 13.01% saved and -0.85262 infections, with zero shelter or special credit. A 9.87% retry by 45398fa33f3f891c earned +0.49350 terminal, -0.90138 infections, +0.01300 shelter, -0.02595 occupied breach and +0.07611 READY coverage. These components explain the ordering in those episodes. They cannot establish that the actor intentionally delays or that every timeout has positive advantage.
* nn_play2.gae assigns each planning decision the full remaining undiscounted shaped return plus any truncation bootstrap. A retry ends with zero bootstrap and a scored terminal component. A timeout has no terminal save reward and uses the critic on a refreshed observation and last recurrent state. Battle discount and lambda use elapsed native simulation time; pauses consume zero discount time. At 12 s, (.99 × .95)^120 = 0.0006354 for a future direct residual. A critic can bridge the interval. The staged longer trace would retain about 0.486, potentially adding variance. Neither number measures an actual causal effect from Firebomb.
* Ordinary sampled PPO stores the chosen tool, conditional cell, both masks, logged probabilities and temperatures. Update replays each complete sequence from its initial recurrent state with the same distribution helpers, sums both log probabilities, globally standardizes advantages, clips the ratio at 0.2 and gradient norm at 0.5. Four randomized passes take one optimizer step per trajectory. Longer battles contribute more samples to global normalization, while each trajectory gets an optimizer step; optional phase weights do not change global normalization. Greedy and exploration-modified discovery actors require separate attribution and must not be relabeled as on-policy PPO samples.
* Last capacity updates showed approximate KL 0.00948/0.03975, clip fractions 0.146/0.304 and explained variance 0.887/-4.841 (128/192). This diagnostic averages per-trajectory pre-step predictions under changing weights across epochs. It is not an out-of-sample critic estimate. A private wide round-six sampled gradient audit had planning/battle recurrent norms 0.033/1.213 and cosine 0.008. This supports checking phase influence and calibration; a single trajectory cannot establish systematic gradient conflict.

## 4. Representation and network capacity

A single GRU consumes a flat 4,889-feature vector and drives linear tool, per-tool cell and value heads. The 8 × 6 grid has HUD/playable/target masks. Geometry endpoints are sequenced through an anchor. Non-spatial actions have a singleton cell where implemented. Cells have individual weights; locality and translation sharing must be learned. The phase flag and shared hidden state permit cross-phase credit but offer no independent critic baseline per phase. Short temporal memory, missing action opportunities and bad spatial inputs are distinguishable hypotheses.

* In four sampled stored real battle frames, median absolute input was zero and the 99th percentile was 3.30–4.84. Raw previous tool reached 114 and 137. make_obs inserts previous tool and cell IDs as ordinary scalar magnitudes. This imposes an artificial numerical ordering on categories. On independent frozen capacity frames, loading exact actor 4cfcaab582ea5746 or db555ca815bce0ba, forwarding the recorded input with the recorded input memory, reproduced tool logits, all conditional cell logits, value and output memory exactly. 75/128 and 114/192 hidden outputs exceeded 0.99 in absolute value. Zeroing only previous tool (114 and 135) shifted the largest raw tool logit by 1.562 and 2.003. This is offline sensitivity on two battle decisions. Test a categorical encoding with explicit migration and a matched control before changing production. No estimate of win-rate impact follows from saturation alone.
* Archived concurrent geometry measurements found 1,581 civilians inside native polygons versus 1,021–1,502 estimated from coarse grids. Exact shape and occupancy therefore carry additional information. Archived five-frame geometry ablation had median tool total variation 0.00214 on the visible arm. Having information, using it in one decision and improving official outcomes each require separate evidence.
* Warm widening from 128 to 192 preserved prior recurrent computation on archived sequence replay per tracker. Training yielded 14 retries/two timeouts versus eight retries/eight timeouts respectively, with zero official finishes. Expansion alone has no demonstrated gameplay advantage. A spatial shared encoder, categorical action history and phase-specific critics are justified **separate** comparisons; evidence does not yet select a replacement architecture.

## Recovery sequence and stopping rules

1. Freeze a manifest before the next comparison: exact actor and parent bytes, Adam state, code/learning revision, feature and mask contract, RNG roles, preparation method, speed, camera, copy, and native session IDs. Retain before-update best actors and after-update snapshots separately. Recover old records by classifying replay, genome-led and standalone planning, then splitting training/discovery/holdout and deduplicating by native outbreak against discovery. Preserve all attempts and failure reasons. A missing native footer, actor or seed stays unknown. The overwritten 12.65% actor cannot be recreated from its successor.
2. Establish the frozen baseline under one measurement contract. Evaluate the preserved standalone actor and the layout-free structured team bf3fc52369fc3746 on separate fresh, discovery-disjoint cohorts at normal speed. Freeze the whole team including base and sampler before evaluation. If its full bundle cannot be recovered, retain its two scores as historical discoveries. Use equal copies and wall budget; rotate copies across arms. Keep previous camera/speed/replay scores as historical strata. Stop any comparison with actor mismatch, seed leakage, inconsistent native receipts or unresolved worker loss. Repair measurement and recollect affected attempts; retain failed attempts in the ledger.
3. Prioritize learned preparation with native data. Record action/mask probability, legal READY deferrals, draft-to-closed polygons, legal budget, occupied civilians, defenses and accepted abilities at transition. Compare a learned hierarchical planner choosing a preparation operation and legal arguments against the current flat tool head. Model owns geometry, budget, completion and READY. Start with read-only supervised prediction of actual native receipts on retained data and held-out seeds. Require calibration and evidence that both arms reach the tested situation. Stop expanding an arm whose signal is absent during a prespecified pilot; an unexposed arm yields no effect estimate.
4. Compare planning target/critic isolation and battle trace length separately from representation changes. Use the same preserved actor/Adam, update budget, sampled policy, observation semantics and native outcomes. Log old/new joint log-probability at update zero, per-phase advantage distributions, held-out critic error by termination, KL/clip by phase, delayed action receipts and count of independent outbreaks. Stop on pre-update replay disagreement, nonfinite targets or missing outcome attribution. Treat the staged 85-test bundled patch as a candidate for a combined experiment only. A combined win would need ablations to assign cause.
5. Treat shadow prediction as a data-quality instrument until held-out actions have support: matched pre-action state, target, delivery time, native spawn/impact, civilian and zombie effect, and multi-step calibration across copies, seeds and policies. The saved shadow benchmark used 322 training windows, one calibration copy and 83 fixed three-step test windows. It supplied future receipt schedules during retrospective evaluation and explicitly refused decision use. Counterfactual action ranking needs independent native intervention evidence or comparably identified variation. Keep learning and promotion in the real game.
6. Use the existing official gate for all candidates and keep the incumbent until the challenger qualifies repeatedly and beats it on fresh held-out campaigns. Report each outcome, minimum/mean, qualifying fraction, retries, errors and unknowns. A four-game-per-arm screen can establish exposure or execution and cannot pass a 30-attempt consistency check. Freeze the candidate before opening a reserved evaluation set. If that set guides tuning, retire it into discovery and collect a new one.

Verification during this audit: 33 isolated shelter-reward, game-control and spatial tests passed. No live actions, training updates, production writes or source-install changes were needed. Archived native sessions that were overwritten and the friend's evaluation remain unavailable.
